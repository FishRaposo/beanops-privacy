What BeanOps does

BeanOps helps an adult organize pregnancy visits, care contacts, questions, and notes. Phase 0A works on one Android phone without a BeanOps account. It is an organizer, not a diagnostic service, emergency service, or source of personalized medical advice.

Your records

You choose what to enter. Records can include names, pregnancy information, due dates, appointments, care contacts, questions, attributed statements, and correction history. The patient and the person recording information can be different people. Record someone else's information only with appropriate permission.

Canonical records are stored in an encrypted database in the app's private storage. Android-protected key storage requires your device authentication. BeanOps does not operate a server that receives these records in Phase 0A. The app has no analytics, advertising, remote crash-reporting, synchronization, or AI service.

Reminders and Android services

Optional Android notifications use generic wording. Android's private scheduling storage contains the notification time and opaque identifiers needed to deliver reminders without opening your encrypted records. Notification permission is optional, and Android can delay delivery. Private records and keys are excluded from automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Manual encrypted backups

You can create a portable encrypted backup protected by a separate password that BeanOps does not store or recover. You choose the destination through Android's document picker. A destination can be another app, removable storage, or a cloud provider, whose own terms and privacy practices apply. BeanOps cannot delete copies held by that provider or recover a lost backup or password. Keep a verified backup somewhere other than only the same phone.

Official references

Opening an official health reference launches your browser. BeanOps does not append your private records to the link. Your browser and the destination website process ordinary connection information under their own policies. Reference links are separate from your private clinician notes.

Corrections, deletion, and retention

Corrections keep revision history until you explicitly delete the record. You can delete individual supported records and their history, or erase the whole local vault. BeanOps does not keep a hidden medical audit copy. Flash storage and compromised-device limits mean forensic erasure cannot be guaranteed. Uninstalling the app removes its private local storage; a portable backup you saved elsewhere can remain.

Deleting records from the app does not change older external backups. Restoring an older backup can bring those records back. You control which external copies to replace or delete.

Security limits

Encryption does not protect records already visible on an unlocked app or on a compromised device. Keep your phone updated and its screen lock secure. BeanOps cannot retrieve your device key or backup password. Support should never require sending an unencrypted medical database or a recovery password.
